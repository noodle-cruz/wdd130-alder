Jacob mumford: how-to-play page.
"What is DO ANYTHING?" DO ANYTHING is an interactive browser-based creative toy where the words "DO ANYTHING" appear on screen and users can grab, drag, sculpt, and physically manipulate the individual letters using their mouse or touch input. The site demonstrates that a webpage can be a medium for pure creative play — no instructions, no menus, just two words that invite the user to explore. The purpose is to delight, surprise, and spark creativity through unexpected interactivity.
"How to Interact" Click and drag around letters. To reset the canvas, reset the site itself. It's that simple!
"Tips & Tricks" Try spinning around letters really fast or pulling apart letters to make a bridge!
"What can You make?" You can DO ANYTHING! People have made hammers, birds, faces, bridges, even rockets!
